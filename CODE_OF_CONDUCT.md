# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This hub helps people discover, use and improve applications. Everyone who takes part — filing an issue,
proposing a change, or answering a question — should be able to do so without harassment or
hostility, regardless of experience level, background, identity or affiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, designs and code — not people.
- Accept that reviewers may decline a change, and explain decisions with reasons.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not post credentials or private files, screenshots, logs, personal
  information or business data. Use synthetic examples and inspect attachments before sharing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive conduct, or persistent unwelcome contact.
- Using project spaces to distribute harmful content or to attack systems you are not authorized to test.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and releases — and when
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via GitHub, or through the private reporting channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled
confidentially and reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict participation, when conduct breaks
these expectations.
